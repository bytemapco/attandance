## Start and stop a class
Role: teacher
Page: /teacher/today
Open Today and press Start on the class. Students can then mark attendance until the window closes. Press Stop to end the class. Stopping marks everyone who did not mark as absent, or excused if they have approved leave.

## Show the QR code
Role: teacher
Page: /teacher/today
After you start a class, open it to show the QR code. The code changes every few seconds, so a photo of it stops working. Students scan it with the app.

## Review flagged attendance
Role: teacher
Page: /teacher/review
A mark is flagged when the face match, the liveness check or the location was doubtful. Open Review, look at the reason and the photo, then accept or reject each one. The student is told your decision.

## Mark or correct attendance by hand
Role: teacher
Page: /teacher/today
Open the class register and change a student's status. After the register is locked you must give a reason, and the change is kept in the audit log.

## Approve a leave request
Role: teacher
Page: /teacher/leaves
Open Leaves to see requests from your students. Approve or reject, with an optional note. An approved leave marks the student excused for classes in those dates.

## Approve a new device
Role: teacher
Page: /teacher/device-changes
A student who changes phone sends a device request. Open Device changes, check it is really the student, then approve or reject.

## See past classes and class analytics
Role: teacher
Page: /teacher/analytics
History lists your finished classes with present and flagged counts. Analytics shows attendance per class and subject so you can see who is falling behind.
